## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A 1 GB whole-route measurement at 2× debits 2 GB once, never once per hop; duplicate/reordered measurements do not double-charge.
- [ ] Every debit retains its applicable multiplier/policy and period; provider IN+OUT is separate; late-data attribution and rounding rules are documented and tested.
- [ ] Fixed monthly day, activation calendar-month anniversary, fixed-day interval and no-auto-reset work in UTC+8; missing dates clamp to month-end then recover the original anchor.
- [ ] Manual reset keeps the next automatic reset anchor; renewal does not reset usage and unused quota does not roll over; history remains queryable.
- [ ] Displayed next reset/timezone/countdown matches the persisted policy; no-reset and unconfigured states never fabricate a countdown.
